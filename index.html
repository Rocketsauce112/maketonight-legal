Privacy Policy for Make Tonight

Effective date: August 29, 2026

Make Tonight ("the App") is developed by Rocketsauce Studios ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

Summary

Make Tonight is an offline, local-first application for iOS and Android. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and the App does not require you to create an account or sign in. The iOS App performs no app-initiated network communication.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the App.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services are used.
  • No advertising SDKs are integrated into the App.
  • We operate no servers and receive no data from the App.

Your Data

All content you create in Make Tonight — including favourites, completed-activity history, personal notes, memories, reminders, and app settings — is stored locally on your device only. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

If you choose to export your data or share a bundled Quick Idea, the App opens the system share or file interface. You decide which app, person, or location receives the content. We do not receive it.

Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored data from your device, subject to your device's own backup or file-management behavior.

Purchases

The iOS edition is a paid download with all features included. It has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, advertising, or separate unlock. Apple processes the App Store transaction; the App does not receive or store your Apple Account or payment information. Apple's handling of the transaction is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

On Android distributions that offer a one-time full-version unlock, the purchase is processed entirely by Google Play. The App asks Google Play whether the purchase exists and stores the resulting entitlement locally. We never receive your name, email address, or payment details. Google's handling of that transaction is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The App requests notification permission only if you switch on optional evening reminders, and uses haptic feedback for timer interactions. Both are optional and used entirely on your device.

Children's Privacy

Since the App does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If Make Tonight's data practices change in the future (for example, if a feature requiring data collection is added), this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly, and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at: rocketsauce.gaming@gmail.com
