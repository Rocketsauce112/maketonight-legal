Privacy Policy for Make Tonight

Effective date: August 21, 2026

Make Tonight ("the App") is developed by Rocketsauce Studios ("we", "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

Summary

Make Tonight is an offline, local-first application. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, and the App does not require you to create an account or sign in. The only network communication the App performs is with Google Play, and only when you choose to buy or restore the one-time full-version unlock.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the App.
  • No analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services are used.
  • No advertising SDKs are integrated into the App.
  • We operate no servers and receive no data from the App.

Your Data

All content you create in Make Tonight — including favourites, completed-activity history, personal notes, memories, reminders, and app settings — is stored locally on your device only, in a local on-device database. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

Uninstalling the App removes this locally stored data from your device, subject to your device's own backup or file-management behavior.

Purchases

The full version is a single one-time in-app purchase. Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. When you buy or restore the purchase, the App asks Google Play whether the purchase exists and stores the resulting entitlement locally on your device so that the full version keeps working offline. We never see or receive your name, email address, or payment details. Google's handling of that transaction is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The App requests notification permission only if you switch on optional evening reminders, and uses vibration for timer feedback. Both are optional and used entirely on your device.

Children's Privacy

Since the App does not collect any personal data from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If Make Tonight's data practices change in the future (for example, if a feature requiring data collection is added), this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly, and the effective date above will be revised.

Contact
